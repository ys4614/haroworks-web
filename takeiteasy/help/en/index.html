Take It Easy Help

It sits at the edge of your desk, counting in silence.

Take It Easy doesn’t make a single sound. It counts out twenty-five minutes, and every so often it leaves a few words on the screen. That’s all it does. Here’s how to get along with it.

Music stays in your music app.

This app doesn’t play background music, and it won’t stop whatever you’re listening to. Music is left to the app you already use. What you can do is hand it a link to a playlist you like, so it’s right there when you sit down to work or step away for a break. Here’s how that goes with Amazon Music.

STEP 1

Copy a link in Amazon Music

  1. Open the playlist, album or song you’d like to keep you company.
  2. Tap the share button. If you can’t find it, Share is tucked inside the “…” menu.
  3. From the list that appears, choose Copy link. On iPhone it may just say Copy.
STEP 2

Paste it into Take It Easy

  1. Go to Settings › Music & words › Music.
  2. Open Music links and tap Paste a copied link to add. The link you just copied drops right in.
  3. Name it whatever you like. “Focus playlist,” “Rainy day,” anything you’ll recognize later.
STEP 3

Open it when you begin

  1. The main screen now shows Open <name>. Tap it and Amazon Music comes up.
  2. When a focus session ends, you’ll also see Open music and start break. Your break begins and the link opens at the same moment.
  3. Once the music is going, come back to Take It Easy. It keeps playing.

It doesn’t have to be Amazon Music. Any app that lets you copy a link from its share menu works the same way. Playlists are fine, and so are albums, single songs and stations. You can save up to five, and pick different music for focus and for breaks.

Your phone decides where a link opens. If the music app is installed, it usually opens there. If it isn’t, or depending on your settings, it may open in the browser instead. If the music doesn’t start on its own, press play in the music app.

Fit the timer to the shape of your day.

A long day working from home and a fifteen-minute lunch break call for different rhythms. Pick a scene in Settings › Timer, and the focus time, short break, long break and number of sessions all change together.

  • Work from home50 min focus · 10 min break · 4 sessions ≈ 4 h 20 min
  • Study25 min focus · 5 min break · 4 sessions ≈ 2 h 10 min (default)
  • Spare moments15 min focus · 3 min break · 3 sessions ≈ 1 h

Names and times can be changed in Add or edit scenes. You can add your own as well, up to five in all. The three that come with the app can always go back with Restore original times.

There isn’t much to touch.

Three buttons. After that, set it on your desk and let it be.

Press play

Press the round button in the middle and a focus session begins. When time’s up, the screen changes to “Nice work!” and you can start your break from there.

Start over, or skip

The left button starts over. The right one moves you along, from focus to break or from break to focus. If the timer is running or paused, it checks with you first, so a stray tap doesn’t cost you anything.

Tap the timer

Some moments you’d rather not see the numbers; other moments you just want a quick look. Tap the timer and the time left appears or disappears for a while. Your settings stay as they are.

Words of encouragement

The words in the middle of the screen change quietly every five minutes or so. If you can’t wait, tap anywhere around them and the next line appears.

Settings

Open it from the gear at the top right. To close it, swipe from left to right. While the timer runs, the gear stays out of sight.

Portrait or landscape

If you stand your phone on the desk like a little clock, landscape feels right.

A buzz and a notice, in place of a sound.

When time is up, Take It Easy lets you know by vibrating and sending a notification. No alarm goes off.

Session-end notifications

The app asks whether it may send notifications the first time you press play, and the timer starts without waiting for your answer. To change your mind later, go to Settings › Notifications. If it says “Notifications are off,” tap Open system settings and allow them there. The notification sound follows your phone’s own settings.

Daily reminder

Once a day, at the time you choose, it gives you a nudge. If you’ve already finished a session that day, it stays quiet.

Focus mode

If other apps keep tapping you on the shoulder, try your phone’s Focus mode. On iPhone, open Control Center, tap Focus and choose Do Not Disturb or Work. On Android, swipe down twice from the top and tap Do Not Disturb. To still get session-end notifications, add Take It Easy to the allowed apps.

Support tips

There are no ads here, and no account to sign up for. If the app has earned a place on your desk, you can send a tip from Settings › Other › Support tip. It helps keep development going.

Each tip is a one-time payment. Paying doesn’t change a single feature, so there’s nothing to restore later.

FAQ

The timer doesn’t make a sound

It was built not to make sound. When time is up, you’ll get a vibration and a notification. If notifications aren’t coming through, have a look at Session-end notifications.

Will it stop my music?

No. The app never touches anything to do with audio, so it can’t stop other apps’ music or turn it down.

It says “No link found”

What you copied doesn’t seem to include a link starting with https://. Copy the link again from your music app’s share menu, then tap Paste a copied link to add once more. If there’s extra text around the link, only the link itself is saved.

The link opens in the browser

Your phone decides which app opens a link. If the music app is installed and allowed to open its links, that’s where it goes. Try reinstalling the music app, or check your phone’s “Open by default” settings.

The screen turns off

While the timer is running on the main screen, the screen stays on. When it’s stopped, or when Settings or another app is open, the screen turns off the way it normally would.

Does the timer keep going while I use other apps?

Yes. The app works out when the session will end and counts toward that moment, so opening other apps or turning off the screen won’t throw it off. When time’s up, a notification lets you know.

Where is my data stored?

Your settings, saved links and past totals stay on your phone and nowhere else. The privacy policy has the details.
