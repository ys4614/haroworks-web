The basics

  • Tap the Play button in the middle to start focusing. When the session ends, a “Nice work!” screen lets you start your break.
  • The left button starts over; the right button skips ahead (focus → break, break → focus). While the timer is running or paused, you’ll be asked to confirm.
  • Tap the timer to show or hide the time left for a while (your settings don’t change).
  • The words of encouragement change quietly every 5 minutes by default. Tap anywhere around the words to see the next one right away.
  • Open Settings with the gear at the top right. Swipe from left to right to close Settings.
  • Works in portrait and landscape. Landscape is nice when the phone stands on your desk.

Adding music links (Amazon Music example)

Take It Easy makes no sound, and it never plays or stops your music. Instead, you can save links to playlists and more from the music app you already use, and open them with one tap when you start focusing or take a break. Here we use Amazon Music as an example.

1. Copy a link in Amazon Music

  1. In the Amazon Music app, open the playlist, album or song you want for focusing or breaks.
  2. Tap the share button (or Share in the “…” menu).
  3. In the share sheet, choose Copy link (on iPhone it may just say Copy).

2. Paste it into Take It Easy

  1. In Take It Easy, open Settings › Music & words › Music.
  2. Open Music links and tap Paste a copied link to add. The link you copied is saved.
  3. Give it a Name such as “Focus playlist” (if you leave it empty, it shows as “List 1” and so on). Tap Try it to check that it opens.
  4. Your first link is chosen as both Focus music and Break music. To use different music for focus and breaks, add another link and choose each one. You can save up to 5 links.

3. Using it

  • The main screen shows Open <name>. Tap it to open the link in Amazon Music. The timer keeps going as it is.
  • When a session ends, the screen shows Open music and start break (or Open music and start focus). Tap it to start the next session and open the link.
  • Once the music is playing, switch back to Take It Easy. Take It Easy never stops your music, so it keeps playing.

Other music apps work the same way. If an app lets you copy a link from its share menu, you can add it with the same steps. Links to playlists, albums, songs, stations and more all work.

Your phone decides how a link opens. If the music app is installed, it usually opens there; otherwise, or depending on your settings, it opens in the browser. If the music doesn’t start, press play in the music app.

Scenes (focus and break times)

Pick a scene in Settings › Timer to set the focus time, short break, long break and sessions before a long break all at once.

  • Work from home: 50 min focus, 10 min break, 4 sessions — about 4 h 20 min
  • Study: 25 min focus, 5 min break, 4 sessions — about 2 h 10 min (the default)
  • Spare moments: 15 min focus, 3 min break, 3 sessions — about 1 h

With Add or edit scenes you can rename scenes, change their times, or add your own (up to 5). Built-in scenes can be reset to their original times.

Notifications, vibration and Focus mode

Session-end notifications

Take It Easy lets you know a session has ended with vibration and a notification. It asks for notification permission the first time you tap Play (the timer starts right away). To change this later, use Session-end notification and Vibration in Settings › Notifications. The notification sound follows your phone’s notification settings.

If you see Notifications are off, tap Open system settings and allow notifications.

Daily reminder

Turn on Daily reminder to get one reminder a day at the time you choose. It’s skipped if you’ve already finished a session that day.

Focus mode (Do Not Disturb)

To keep other apps’ notifications from distracting you, use your phone’s Focus mode.

  • iPhone: open Control Center → Focus → choose Do Not Disturb or Work (turn it off there when done)
  • Android: swipe down twice from the top of the screen → tap Do Not Disturb in Quick Settings (the name varies by phone)

To still get session-end notifications, add Take It Easy to the allowed apps of your Focus mode.

Display and themes

  • Face: digital / hourglass / analog
  • Style: clear (an easy-to-read “n min left”) / modern (thin digits and ticks)
  • Show time left: on or off for each face
  • Theme: four colors — Morning / Forest / Night / Paper. Under Appearance, choose light, dark (pure black) or match your phone
  • Words of encouragement (Settings › Music & words): how often the words change

Support tips

Take It Easy has no ads and needs no account. If you enjoy it, you can support development from Settings › Other › Support tip (a coffee / lunch / a treat). Each tip is a one-time payment. Tips don’t change any features, and there is nothing to restore.

FAQ

The timer doesn’t make a sound

Take It Easy is a silent app. It tells you a session has ended with vibration and a notification. If notifications don’t arrive, see Notifications, vibration and Focus mode.

Will it stop my music?

No. Take It Easy doesn’t use any audio features, so it never stops or lowers music from other apps.

It says “No link found”

What you copied doesn’t contain a link (starting with https://). In your music app, use Share → Copy link, then tap Paste a copied link to add again. If there is other text around the link, only the link is saved.

The link opens in the browser

Your phone decides how links open. A link opens in the music app when the app is installed and allowed to open such links. Try reinstalling the music app or checking your phone’s “Open by default” settings.

The screen turns off

The screen stays on while the timer is running on the main screen. When the timer is stopped, or while Settings or another app is open, the screen turns off as your phone normally does.

Does the timer keep going while I use other apps?

Yes. It counts from the time a session will end, so it stays accurate even when another app is open or the screen is off. A notification tells you when the session ends.

Where is my data stored?

Your settings, saved links and totals are stored only on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.
