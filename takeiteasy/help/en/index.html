Your music, one tap away.

Take It Easy makes no sound, and it never plays or stops your music. Instead, save links from the music app you already use and open them right when you start focusing or take a break. Here we use Amazon Music as an example.

STEP 1

Copy a link in Amazon Music

  1. Open the playlist, album or song you want
  2. Tap the share button (or Share in the “…” menu)
  3. Choose Copy link (on iPhone it may just say Copy)
STEP 2

Paste it into Take It Easy

  1. Open Settings › Music & words › Music
  2. Music links › Paste a copied link to add
  3. Give it a clear name, like “Focus playlist”
STEP 3

Open it as you start

  1. Tap Open <name> on the main screen to open Amazon Music
  2. When a session ends, there’s also Open music and start break
  3. Once the music plays, just switch back to Take It Easy

Other music apps work the same way. If an app lets you copy a link from its share menu, you can add it with the same steps. Links to playlists, albums, songs, stations and more all work. Save up to 5 links, and pick different ones for focus and breaks if you like.

Your phone decides how a link opens. If the music app is installed, it usually opens there; otherwise, or depending on your settings, it opens in the browser. If the music doesn’t start, press play in the music app.

Just pick a scene.

Choose a scene in Settings › Timer to set focus, break, long break and sessions all at once.

  • Work from home50 min focus · 10 min break · 4 sessions ≈ 4 h 20 min
  • Study25 min focus · 5 min break · 4 sessions ≈ 2 h 10 min (default)
  • Spare moments15 min focus · 3 min break · 3 sessions ≈ 1 h

With Add or edit scenes you can rename scenes, change their times, or add your own (up to 5). Built-in scenes can be reset to their original times.

The basics

Three buttons. Then just leave it on your desk.

Play to start

The middle button starts a focus session. When it ends, a “Nice work!” screen lets you start your break.

Start over, skip

Left starts over; right skips ahead (focus → break, break → focus). While running or paused, you’ll be asked to confirm.

Tap the timer

Tap the timer to show or hide the time left for a while. Your settings don’t change.

Words of encouragement

They change quietly every 5 minutes by default. Tap anywhere around the words to see the next one.

Settings

Open with the gear at the top right; swipe from left to right to close. The gear hides while the timer runs.

Portrait or landscape

Standing your phone on the desk like a clock? Landscape works great.

Vibration and a notification. No sound.

When a session ends, Take It Easy lets you know with vibration and a notification.

Session-end notifications

Permission is asked the first time you tap Play (the timer starts right away). Switch notifications and vibration in Settings › Notifications. If you see “Notifications are off”, tap Open system settings. The notification sound follows your phone’s settings.

Daily reminder

Get one reminder a day at the time you choose. It’s skipped if you’ve already finished a session that day.

Focus mode

iPhone: Control Center › Focus › Do Not Disturb or Work. Android: swipe down twice › Do Not Disturb. To still get session-end notifications, add Take It Easy to the allowed apps.

FAQ

The timer doesn’t make a sound

Take It Easy is a silent app. It tells you a session has ended with vibration and a notification. If notifications don’t arrive, see Session-end notifications.

Will it stop my music?

No. Take It Easy doesn’t use any audio features, so it never stops or lowers music from other apps.

It says “No link found”

What you copied doesn’t contain a link (starting with https://). In your music app, use Share → Copy link, then tap Paste a copied link to add again. If there is other text around the link, only the link is saved.

The link opens in the browser

Your phone decides how links open. A link opens in the music app when the app is installed and allowed to open such links. Try reinstalling the music app or checking your phone’s “Open by default” settings.

The screen turns off

The screen stays on while the timer is running on the main screen. When the timer is stopped, or while Settings or another app is open, the screen turns off as your phone normally does.

Does the timer keep going while I use other apps?

Yes. It counts from the time a session will end, so it stays accurate even when another app is open or the screen is off. A notification tells you when the session ends.

Where is my data stored?

Your settings, saved links and totals are stored only on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.
